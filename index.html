Digital Forensics

System Analysis & Evidence Recovery

Project Overview

Digital forensics is the vital process of uncovering, interpreting, and preserving electronic data. In a world increasingly driven by digital footprints, this discipline is crucial for investigating cybercrimes, recovering lost data, and understanding complex digital systems.


This project explores the foundational tools, operating systems, and methodologies used by modern forensic analysts to secure and trace electronic evidence across networks and isolated devices, all presented on this refined, research documentation platform.
